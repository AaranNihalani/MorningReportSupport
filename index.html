Why we built this

Millions of young people get their news from an algorithm optimized for attention, not accuracy.

We've seen firsthand how misleading, biased, or simply false information spreads through social media. The Morning Report gives you the "news will find me" convenience you're used to — without handing your understanding of the world over to an influencer.

Every headline is sourced from reputable outlets and linked back to the original article, so you can form your own opinions, dig deeper into what interests you, and build the media literacy that matters in every area of life.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this just for investors?

Not at all. Every report leads with general headlines covering world events, politics and trends — your stock watchlist is one personalized section within it, not the whole product.

Where does the news come from?

We aggregate stories from thousands of reputable news outlets and market data providers. Every headline links back to the original source so you can verify it yourself.

Is the app free?

Yes, The Morning Report is free to download and use. We are supported by privacy-conscious advertising that keeps our servers running.

How do I manage my watchlist?

Navigate to the "Watchlist" tab in the app. You can add stocks by symbol (e.g., AAPL) or company name, and remove them with a swipe.

How do I contact support?

You can email us directly at themorningreportinitiative@gmail.com. We typically respond within 24 hours.

Can my school or organization partner with you?

Yes — we're actively looking to partner with schools and educators to help students build financial and media literacy. Reach out and let's talk.
